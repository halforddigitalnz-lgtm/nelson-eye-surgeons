Optometrist or GP referral

Whilst a referral from an Optometrist or registered medical practitioner is preferred, it is not essential. However, if your query relates to either dry or watery eyes, we ask that you please ensure you have read and followed our helpful guidance which is available to you for free on the Patient Information page. If symptoms persist, you will then need to consult your community optometrist as a first port-of-call.
